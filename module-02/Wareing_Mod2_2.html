Assignment 2.2 – Building a Web Page Exercise, Part 2

Chapter One: Finding the Right Track

I live in California and I am working toward a Bachelor of Science in Software Development at Bellevue University. Most of my coursework so far has been in Python, databases, and now front-end development. I picked this program because it lets me build things instead of only reading about them.

I did not start out in technology. My first jobs were in retail and at a movie theater, which taught me more about troubleshooting under pressure than I expected at the time. Explaining a product to someone who has never heard of it turns out to be good practice for explaining a bug to someone who has never seen the code. Those years are the reason I care about making things clear rather than clever.

Outside of school I spend a lot of time gaming, which is what pulled me toward computers in the first place. I have gotten into modding and building small tools around the games I play, and that hobby has a way of turning into practice without feeling like it. Lately I have been putting the same energy into side projects that have nothing to do with games at all.

My goal after graduation is to work in security, starting as a SOC analyst and moving toward penetration testing. I have earned the ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity credential and finished the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate to build a foundation while I finish the degree. Web development might seem like a detour from that path, but knowing how a page is actually put together makes it much easier to understand how one breaks.
